# Weighoto

**Weight loss, explained simply — for real life in the UK.**

Weighoto is a small UK team writing about weight loss in plain English. No crash diets, no miracle products — just honest guides on eating well, moving more, sleeping better, and building habits that actually stick.

## What we write about

- Simple, sustainable eating — no banned food groups, no misery
- Everyday movement — walking, stairs, and habits you can keep
- Sleep and stress — the hidden half of weight loss
- Tracking progress the sensible way — trends over weeks, not one bad day

## One small thing that confuses a lot of people

In the UK, clinic notes and scales talk in kilograms, while family and friends talk in stone and pounds — so the same weight can look like three different numbers. When that happens, a quick check on a [kg to stone and pounds](https://kgtostoneandpounds.uk) converter puts everything in the same language in seconds.
